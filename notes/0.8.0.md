- **Ready at its prompt from the start.** A Vibe session that was just opened or resumed and is waiting at its prompt can be compacted or cleared, and woken by a message, without anyone typing in it first. Before, that only worked after its first turn.
- **A command that starts no turn no longer leaves it stuck.** After a line like `/help`, the session counts as back at its prompt after a few quiet seconds, so Compact, Clear and message waking work again.
