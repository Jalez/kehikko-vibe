- **Mistral Vibe on your kehikko.** A terminal holding the Vibe conversation that belongs to the epic, the parts and the kehikko you are looking at, resumed when you come back to it.
- **It knows where it is.** A new conversation starts out knowing its place, the modules on the kehikko and the skills it was given, and hears what changed as it works.
- **Working, waiting and finished in your Inbox.** Vibe's own status tells Kehikot when a conversation is working, waiting for your approval or answer, or done; research it finishes gets its own sound.
- **Kehikot's doors and skills, for that conversation alone.** Each conversation gets the kehikko's MCP servers and its modules' skills as slash commands, and nothing is written into your Vibe settings.
